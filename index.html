About Us

MediRoute

Our Mission

MediRoute is an AI-powered emergency healthcare platform designed to connect patients with the nearest available hospitals in seconds. In critical moments, every second counts — and MediRoute ensures you never waste one searching for help.

What We Do

We use real-time hospital availability data, distance calculations, and cost filters to intelligently match patients with the most suitable hospital for their emergency. From ICU beds to general wards and ambulance dispatch, MediRoute covers it all.

Our Vision

We envision a world where no one is turned away from emergency care due to lack of information. MediRoute bridges the gap between patients in distress and the healthcare infrastructure that can save their lives.

How It Works

MediRoute makes emergency hospital access simple in 4 easy steps.

1
Trigger Emergency or Explore

Tap the red Emergency button for immediate assistance, or browse hospitals freely using the Explore option. Enter your location and preferences.

2
AI Filters Hospitals

Our system instantly filters hospitals based on real-time bed availability, your distance, cost preference (Low / Mid / High), and required service type (ICU or General Bed).

3
Book Your Hospital

Select a hospital from the results and tap "Book Hospital". Confirm your booking in the popup — it takes under 10 seconds.

4
Track Your Ambulance

After booking, you're taken to the live ambulance tracking screen showing driver details, vehicle ID, estimated arrival time, and real-time status.
